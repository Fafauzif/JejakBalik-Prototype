## Interpretation controls

- Borrow hierarchy and interaction patterns from Shopify, not its black top bar, commerce semantics, or product identity.
- Borrow miniature geometry and useful inspectors from the logistics images, not their blue brand palette or screenshot text overlays.
- Borrow room/cutaway readability from management-game references; keep Jejak Balik professional and less visually crowded.
- Borrow useful comparison hierarchy from Stripe/dashboard images; keep the approved warm palette and operational metric definitions.
- The supplied agent constellation is inspiration for composition and expressive character scale. Generate original Jejak Balik characters, not copies of its exact mascots or studio identities.
- The technical-stack screenshot and actual Sudirman station photos from the old archive are deliberately excluded: they are not visual requirements for this fictional city build.
