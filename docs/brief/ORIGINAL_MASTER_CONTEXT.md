## 4. Concept and business boundary

Jejak Balik is an asset-light, free-to-household circular reverse-logistics platform for household/community e-waste. Households schedule collection windows. Partner-operated backhaul, route-compatible pickup, and grouped reverse routes consolidate requests. The platform records assets and custody, coordinates capacity, supports triage/disposition, and returns visible outcomes.

The startup owns the coordination layer, customer interface, asset/network twin, classification rules, partner standards, and reporting. It does not need its own fleet or recycling plant and does not perform chemical recycling or hazardous-material processing. Government is not its operator. Warungs, informal workers, data centers, GPUs, and server infrastructure are not the current core scope.

Value pathways are reuse, repair, refurbishment, parts recovery, material recovery, and specialist treatment. Data-bearing status creates a clearance gate before release and is not an extra disposition. Uncertain/safety-sensitive cases require human operator review. Downstream economics can be positive or negative; not every collected item generates revenue.

Funding combines service contracts, community/sponsor access support, and recovered-value sharing. Standard household pickup is free and scheduled, not instant everywhere. The fixture model shows included batch operating contribution, not actual company profit. No commercial agreement or real price quotation is implied.
